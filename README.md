# King Wa Tam, PhD

PhD-trained researcher with over a decade of research experience in epidemiology,
statistics, health economics and quantitative social science, from Australia to Hong
Kong to Canada, plus consulting work for the World Bank.

I enjoy building agentic AI software in my spare time, where much of research still
applies: frame the right question before the method, and treat a confident answer with
the same suspicion as a significant p-value. Recent formal training in AI agents and
generative AI sits in [my certificates](https://kingwatam.github.io/certificates).

## Research repositories

Check out the repositories below for my work history. Each provides detailed
documentation in its README describing the projects.

- [wb_griffith](https://github.com/kingwatam/wb_griffith): disease burden modelling
  for health tax policy across eight countries and 48 Sub-Saharan African countries
  (The World Bank and Griffith University, 2022-2026)
- [cepr_ucalgary](https://github.com/kingwatam/cepr_ucalgary): late mortality and
  subsequent primary neoplasms among childhood cancer survivors (UCalgary, 2024-2025)
- [sphpc_cuhk](https://github.com/kingwatam/sphpc_cuhk): community eHealth,
  multimorbidity, mental health and health-services research (CUHK, 2020-2024)
- [csrp_hku](https://github.com/kingwatam/csrp_hku): school mental health, suicide
  epidemiology, poverty and health services (HKU, 2019-2020)
- [sph_uq](https://github.com/kingwatam/sph_uq): energy balance, risk factor
  distribution prediction and Markov Monte Carlo simulation of health burden
  (UQ, 2014-2022)
